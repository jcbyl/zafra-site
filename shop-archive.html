Our Story

How it works

We source directly from farmer-owned cooperatives in PR's central highlands. The farmer gets paid first. Then we curate the best of the island.

1

Our Harvest

Coffee, cacao, honey, vanilla — grown by PR farmers, processed by farmer-owned co-ops, bought at $25/almud (government floor: $18).

2

Island Partners

We curate the best PR-made products — chocolate makers, cigar rollers, soap artisans, hot sauce crafters, wood carvers. Every product has a maker and a story.

3

Mail It Home

Pick what you love. We pack it in a Zafra green-and-gold gift box and ship it to your door. Every order includes a provenance card.
