## 5. Pricing Strategy

**Positioning:** Premium — above every PR competitor, below ultra-luxury import brands.

**Anchor prices:**
- 12oz coffee: $22 (SOBAO ~$8, Don Ruiz ~$14-18, specialty mainland $18-24)
- 8oz honey: $18 (Apiarios Caraballo ~$10-12, mass organic ~$12-18)
- 3 vanilla beans: $18 (market rate ~$200/kg = ~$18-25 for 3 beans)
- 8oz cacao: $16 (no PR comparable, specialty cacao $12-20)

**Gift set margin:** PR Terroir Box at $65 — components cost ~$35-40, margin ~40%.

**Subscription margin:** $55/mo for 12oz coffee + shipping — highest margin channel (direct-to-consumer, no wholesale discount).
