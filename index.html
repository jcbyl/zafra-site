How the Money Comes Home

Our standard: we buy at the farm gate, at or above the government floor. Where a farmer can’t find pickers, we’re building crews that come with the purchase — the farmer pays nothing for the harvest and keeps the full price.

The crews are funded by a share of every Zafra sale and by people who want to help bring the harvest in.

The standard every lot is held to: ripeness at intake. Fermentation logged. Optical sorting. QR lot codes from block to bag. Every bag carries a provenance card — region, farm, harvest date, process — and the price paid to the farmer, with the farm and the farmer on the front label.
