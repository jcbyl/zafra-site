The Story

Puerto Rico once fed the world's coffee cups. After generations of decline and then Hurricane Maria, the island grows only a fraction of the coffee it drinks. The farmers who stayed have the land, the heirloom trees and the knowledge. What they lack is a way to the market.

Zafra is that way. We buy direct, pay fairly, and carry the harvest to buyers who value where it comes from.

The Four Harvests

Each harvest has its own history and its own reason for being rare.

Coffee · Yauco · Jayuya · Adjuntas · Maricao

Coffee

Heirloom Arabica from the mountains of Yauco, Jayuya, Adjuntas and Maricao. Washed process, small lots, grown at altitude.

Our commitment: pay above the government floor — and show you the price paid, on every lot.
Cacao · Heritage Trinitario

Cacao

Heritage Trinitario descended from Spanish colonial plantings — the fine-flavor type, from trees isolated on the island for generations.

Fermented, sun-dried, and sold as single-origin beans for chocolate makers.
Honey · Cordillera Central

Honey

Raw, unfiltered wildflower and coffee-blossom honey from the Cordillera Central. Mountain-grown and untreated.

Organic certification is something we are pursuing — not yet held.
Vanilla · Castañer · USDA-TARS · Since 1909

Vanilla

USDA-TARS varieties from Castañer, a crop the island has grown since 1909. Zafra plans to buy directly from the Cooperativa de Vainilla de Puerto Rico, a farmer cooperative founded in 2023, and carry its vanilla to market.

Working toward a supply partnership. No contract executed yet — the plan, stated plainly.
