# Zafra Trading Co. — Design Taste Library

## Reference Sites

### Onyx Coffee Lab (onyxcoffeelab.com)
- Cream background (#fbfaf3), product photography as primary visual. Minimal text — product speaks first.
### Stumptown Coffee (stumptowncoffee.com)
- Full-bleed hero, warm cream, GT Flexa type. Generous whitespace. Consistent card image heights.
### Coffee Bros. (coffeebros.com)
- Montserrat sans-serif, white background, text-only nav. Product-first layout. Short descriptions.
### Guerlain (guerlain.com)
- Luxury single-product hero, dark sections breaking light, serif+sans pairing.

## Design Decisions
- Palette: Cream (#faf8f3), forest green (#1a3a24), gold (#c4a04a). No brown.
- Type: Inter (body) + Cormorant Garamond (headings)
- Contrast: All text ≥4.5:1. ink-mute #5a6a5e (5.4:1 on cream, 5.7:1 on white)
- Layout: Cream throughout, dark green only for CTA + footer
