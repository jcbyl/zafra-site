Provenance

Every shipment carries its card.

Not a story on a label — a record in the box. Each shipment from this house includes a provenance card stating exactly where your harvest came from and what the farmer was paid.

i.

Region

The municipality and mountain the harvest came from — Yauco, Maricao, the cordillera.

ii.

Farm & Co-op

The named farm or cooperative that grew it. No anonymous blends.

iii.

Harvest Date

When it was picked, when it was milled, when it was roasted.

iv.

Farmer Price

What the grower was paid, printed on the card. Direct trade, on the record.
